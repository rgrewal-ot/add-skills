# brainstorming

1. Restate the question in one line and ask one clarifying question if the goal is unclear. Otherwise start.
2. Give three to five options that are genuinely different from each other (not variations of one idea). For each: one line what it is, one line what it costs or risks, one line when it is the right choice.
3. Say which option you would pick for this repo and why, in two lines. Refer to `spec.md` and `AGENTS.md` if they exist.
4. Ask the user to pick or combine. Do not implement anything in this skill; the chosen option goes into `/plan` or an issue.

Rules: no real or productive data; keep the whole answer under one screen.
